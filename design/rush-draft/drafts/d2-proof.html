NotesBefore you ask

I'm not a tech major. Can I still apply?

Yes. We take every major. Curiosity about technology is the bar, not a transcript.

I'm a sophomore. Is it too late?

No. Freshmen and sophomores are exactly who we recruit.

What's the time commitment?

Pledging scales with what you want out of it. Expect a few hours a week during the process; after that, it's yours to set.
